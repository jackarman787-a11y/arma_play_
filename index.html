📜 ARMA PLAY Rules & Privacy

1. Data Collection

Hum aapka email ya phone number sirf login verification ke liye use karte hain. Aapka data kisi third-party ko nahi becha jata.

2. Data Saving

Jab aap Data Saving mode on karte hain, toh hum audio bitrate kam kar dete hain taaki aapka mobile data bache. Offline mode mein sirf downloaded songs play honge.

3. Content Rules

ARMA PLAY par kisi bhi prakar ka illegal, copyright-infringing, ya offensive content upload karna strictly mana hai. Aisa karne par account ban kiya ja sakta hai.

4. Account Security

Apna password aur OTP kisi ke saath share na karein. Agar aapko koi suspicious activity dikhe, toh turant support se contact karein.

5. Fair Usage

Automated bots ya scripts ka use karke songs download karna ya stream karna allowed nahi hai. Server ko theek rakhne ke liye fair usage policy apply hoti hai.
