📜 ARMA PLAY Rules & Privacy

1. Data Collection

We use your email or phone number strictly for login verification. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

2. Data Saving & Offline Mode

When you enable Data Saving mode, we reduce the audio bitrate to help you save mobile data. In Offline Mode, only downloaded songs will be playable.

3. Content Rules

Uploading or sharing any illegal, copyright-infringing, or offensive content on ARMA PLAY is strictly prohibited. Violations will result in account bans.

4. Account Security

Never share your password or OTP with anyone. If you notice any suspicious activity, contact support immediately.

5. Fair Usage Policy

The use of automated bots or scripts to download or stream songs is not allowed. We implement fair usage policies to keep the server stable for everyone.
